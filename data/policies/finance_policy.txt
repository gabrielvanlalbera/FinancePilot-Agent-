FINANCE POLICY
Version: 1.0

1. TRANSACTION MATCHING
A bank transaction may be considered a valid match only when:
- The vendor matches the invoice vendor.
- The transaction reference matches the invoice ID.
- The transaction amount exactly matches the invoice amount.

2. AUTOMATIC PROCESSING
Transactions of ₹50,000 or less may be automatically processed when:
- A valid invoice match exists.
- There is no amount mismatch.
- The invoice status is Approved.

3. HUMAN APPROVAL
Any transaction greater than ₹50,000 requires human approval before it can be processed.

4. AMOUNT MISMATCH
If the bank transaction amount differs from the invoice amount:
- Do not automatically process the transaction.
- Mark it as Mismatch.
- Investigate the difference.
- Request human review if the reason cannot be established.

5. MISSING INVOICE
If a bank transaction does not have a corresponding invoice:
- Do not process the transaction.
- Mark it as Needs Review.
- Report the missing invoice.

6. VERIFICATION
Every transaction processed by FinancePilot must be independently verified after the ERP update.

7. AUDITABILITY
FinancePilot must record:
- The task performed.
- Actions taken.
- Decisions made.
- Tool results.
- Errors and recovery attempts.
- Human approvals.
- Final verification result.

8. SAFETY
FinancePilot must never:
- Modify an invoice without authorization.
- Process a transaction when required information is missing.
- Bypass the human approval requirement.
- Mark a transaction as completed without successful verification.
